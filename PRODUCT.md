# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):

- **Recruiters and interviewers** at quant trading firms and tech companies deciding whether to interview Adam. They often skim in a minute or two, frequently from a link in a resume or application.
- **Technical peers**: engineers and quant hobbyists who want to dig into the data, the method and the code.

Both should be able to explore hands-on; the site serves a fast skim path and a deep path on the same pages.

## Product Purpose

A public showcase for an exchange-style limit order book and matching engine (Python and C++17), validated on a full day of raw Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH data, with machine-learning research on top. The site exists so visitors **explore the work hands-on** (confirmed goal): scrub through Apple's real order book on 2019-01-30, and try market-making strategies and ML quote filters against real order flow. Success is a visitor who has played with the replay and the playground and comes away trusting the results.

## Positioning

Real exchange data, end to end, with honest evaluation: the book is rebuilt from 368M raw Nasdaq messages with 0 of 198M order references missed, and every strategy number on the site is a real backtest with settings chosen before seeing the test period. A held-out second day (2019-12-30) is reported even where results got worse.

## Operating Context

- Static pages served from the repo's `docs/` folder on GitHub Pages and Vercel (`orderbook-eosin.vercel.app`).
- `docs/index.html` is generated by `cpp/itch/make_viewer.py` from `cpp/itch/viewer_template.html`, with the AAPL book and trade data embedded.
- `docs/playground.html` loads `docs/playground_data.json`, written by `backtest/ml_backtest.py`.
- Visitors arrive from a resume, application, LinkedIn or GitHub, on desktop and phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Replay: a 2019-01-30 AAPL session with a 10-level depth ladder and trade tape, scrubbable through the day.
- Playground: precomputed backtests for filters (none, order-book imbalance, ML next-move, latency-trained ML, fill-toxicity ML), activity rates (10–60%), latencies (0, 100 µs, 1 ms, 10 ms), pull vs. step back, and quote placement; model diagnostics (AUC, ROC, feature importance); generalisation (other stocks, a second day, SPY); C++ inference timing.
- Every number on the site must come from the generated data files; nothing is typed in by hand.
- Terminology: order book, matching engine, depth ladder, trade tape, markout, adverse selection, imbalance, AUC, latency, pull rate / activity rate.

## Product Principles

1. Exploration first: the visitor's hands should be on the data within one screen.
2. Every claim is checkable: numbers link back to the data, method and code behind them.
3. Honest over impressive: show the null and negative results next to the wins.
4. Fast to grasp, deep to explore: the skim path and the deep path live on the same page.
